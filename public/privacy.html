The important details

A clear, simple privacy note.

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This notice describes how SK Wholesaler handles information submitted through its newsletter and contact features. Please review and adapt it to the business and the places where you sell.

Information you provide

When you contact us using the contact form, your name, email, subject, and message are stored so we can respond. If you subscribe to the newsletter, your email is stored for that purpose. When you choose to order through WhatsApp, the items and message you send are handled by WhatsApp and are not submitted to this website.

How information is used

Information submitted through this website is used to respond to inquiries and manage newsletter subscriptions. WhatsApp messages are handled by WhatsApp and the store operator. Access to the admin area is intended for authorized store staff.

Storage and retention

Store data is held in the database configured by the operator. This starter project does not define a hosting provider, automatic backups, or a retention schedule. The store operator must secure the database, limit access, configure backups, and decide how long records are kept.

Cookies and browser storage

The storefront saves your cart and wishlist in your browser's local storage. Admin sign-in uses an HTTP-only session cookie. Clear local browser storage to remove saved cart and wishlist data; sign out of the admin area to end its session.

Your choices and questions

To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information, contact the store team. Applicable rights and response timelines depend on where you live and where the store operates.
